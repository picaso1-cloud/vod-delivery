Adaptive quality for the TV player.
- Every TV gets its own ladder: copy → 1080p → 720p → 480p → 360p. If a title stalls or errors, the server restarts the same position one step lower automatically (the TV shows "Switching to 720p…").
- The highest profile that played is remembered per TV, so the next cast starts there. After 3 clean plays it probes one step up; a failed probe blocks upgrades for 6 hours.
- TV-player transcodes are now MKV instead of MPEG-TS — the TS container stalled on the Allwinner SC_96Q even at 360p, while MKV 1080p plays. DLNA casting still uses TS.
- Fixed the first fetch of a growing transcode declaring a tiny Content-Length (the player saw a 10 KB "file" and stopped).
- The TV app ignores the transient HTTP error while switching and shows the switching state.
