TV player refinements.
- The title overlay fades after 4 seconds and reappears on any D-pad press.
- New Tracks panel (Down on the remote): audio tracks with language/codec/channels and subtitle tracks including "Subtitles off". Navigate with Up/Down, select with OK, close with Back.
- Server transcodes for TV players downmix to stereo AAC (5.1 sources failed on older Allwinner chipsets).
- The TV client ships in the same APK: this release is published as both vodcast-v0.3.8.apk (phone) and vodcast-tv-v0.3.8.apk (TV).
